About 6 weeks ago, I ran [Outpost](https://outpost.hackclub.com), a 4-day hardware hackathon, which then led into a 20-booth expo at [Open Sauce](https://opensauce.com)! We brought roughly 120 teenagers there, who all had to build hardware projects to qualify.

![image](https://cdn.hackclub.com/01a078cc-ab00-7a61-8eb8-c64416077b92/paste-1788733071835.png)

<div class="caption"> Super cool event, and I'll have to write about it more later! </div>

Anyhow, while we ran the hackathon portion at Frontier Tower, we _almost_ ran it at [Forbes Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forbes_Island) instead, which is out in the [Sacramento River Delta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sacramento%E2%80%93San_Joaquin_River_Delta).

We ended up deciding against it because it wasn't the bet we wanted to take with the deliverable of 20 Open Sauce booths looming over us, however it _was_ still a kick-ass venue and we still very much wanted to run an event there! So my co-conspirator Indy and I ended up deciding to take this past weekend to scope out the venue and see what exactly the shape of the island was. Consider this blog post a bit of a status report; I'll **bold** any learnings I want to highlight

The premise was simple: Fly to SFO, get over to Antioch, take the ferry to Bradford Island, walk up to Forbes Island, camp for 5 days, and then get back.

<div class="caption"> first we'd take the yellow line up to Antioch...</div>

![image](https://cdn.hackclub.com/01a078be-dfee-7eb0-8153-b6539ef6e0e1/paste-1788732169000.png)

## Day 1

I flew out of BTV at 6:00 AM to JFK on Wednesday, and then landed in SFO at roughly 12PM PST. It was not a fun flight. I think I'm growing old.

![image](https://cdn.hackclub.com/01a078da-f711-7316-937a-0946bcb80732/paste-1788734010342.png)

<div class="caption"> I caught this while boarding the plane. Good ol' linux </div>

Anyhow, I then went to the place where I was crashing in SF to drop my stuff off, and by the time all was said and done it was roughly 2:00. Indy called me from a boat and we started discussing gameplan; we decided that I should just start making my way to Antioch after getting food and supplies.

![image](https://cdn.hackclub.com/01a078ee-1d88-7926-aaf9-899df5b8d479/paste-1788735264793.png)

This leads us to blunder #1: I got lunch nearby 16th and mission, but then instead of waiting to get to Antioch before getting supplies, I saw a Costco & other supermarkets nearby and figured it would be more efficient to do a quick supply run before hopping on the BART.

Turns out this is not true! The extra energy and slowdowns of having to carry groceries everywhere ended up adding almost an **hour and a half** of time to our commute, and it would've been way faster just to meet at Antioch and for us to speedrun our groceries from there, and I ended up having to return the items anyways. **Close distance with your teammates whenever possible**. The theoretical time gains were not worth it.

Anyhow, I eventually got to Antioch at 6:00PM PST, where I then called Indy and went on a Costco run for supplies. We set a hard deadline of 8PM to get myself out to the ferrypoint with all the stuff, which really meant just over an hour to get to Costco and buy everything we would need to survive for 5 days. Here's what I bought:

![image](https://cdn.hackclub.com/01a078e6-d735-7e81-a904-b124df664b76/paste-1788734788594.png)

Pictured:

- Bread (4 bags of bagels, 1 loaf of bread, 1 box of Croissants)
- Fruit (4 bags of plums, 2 bags of navel oranges, 2 clusters of bananas, 1 bag of apples, and 1 bag of clementines )

- and a singular jar of peanut butter for good measure

I then took an Uber to get to the agreed-upon [ferrypoint](https://maps.app.goo.gl/pXufg81yvSREZS7r6), where Indy would pick me up via boat, but we were met with a gate that blocked us from getting there a few miles out (aside: I _really_ wish I took a picture of this! It had curfew schedules that aren't public).

_Shoot_ I thought, I don't know where to go. I called Indy and after a bit of discussion we ended up rerouting to [The Rusty Porthole](https://maps.app.goo.gl/suXC6nBFktimRoXE7) instead, which happens to have a marina he could pick me up from.

![](https://cdn.hackclub.com/01a078f7-4c35-7773-bf84-fe0cd73359ac/paste-1788735866678.png)

<div class="caption"> the new pickup point! </div>

I ended up having to wait about an hour here, since Indy was trying to figure out what boat he could commandeer to actually grab me. I got a soda in the meantime and hung out for a bit. Got it for free!

![image](https://cdn.hackclub.com/01a078f8-8971-7b4c-aed3-6439bd32d174/paste-1788735947553.png)

It was nigthtime by the time he came around, and while I'm not sure what I expected, he arrived in what was basically a glorified half-canoe with a motor strapped to the back of it. I wish I took pictures of this thing, it was ridiculous

Anyways, after a bit of a hectic load-in, off we went to this island!

Surprisingly though, after about and hour, we finally made it to our destination!

## Day 2

The next day it was Thursday; it was mostly uneventful, I mainly spent the day doing some Hack Club work, and also shipping a [new website](https://hackclub.dari.zone/) with some of my Hack Club philosophy documents, since it was scattered around various PDFs in the slack and repositories on GitHub. I was pretty happy with it.

Around noon I decided to go out and explore a bit with Indy. I looked around the various boats parked in the area (one of which had a piano on it somehow, so I played that for a bit), and then went out to the ranch nearby.

While we were outside, I saw there was a lot of empty road and remarked how nice it would be if there was a bike I could steal. Literaly moments later, we spotted a bike in a ditch and pulled it out. After a couple of test turns it seemed fine, so I rode around on it for an hour.

![image](https://cdn.hackclub.com/01a07916-398c-7be8-ab05-adf7d85a37de/paste-1788737892114.png)

<div class="caption"> this thing worked surprisingly well! the handles turned my hands black but generally it didn't lock up or anything </div>

During this entire day there was a dog named Hugo that really had it out for me. I took it as a slow day and spent more of it inside, because whenever I would go outside it would just start barking at me and chasing at me. Mike (the owner) could not get him to calm down no matter what.

When it was evening, Mike locked Hugo up so I decided to come back outside and talk with him a bit; Mike was there when Indy and I got to the island, but I didn't really chat with him all day apart from helping him find a thing or two as he was packing.

Turns out, Mike was really cool! He taught me how to fish, and then we ended up just chatting for awhile. I'll spare details, but he went through some _crazy_ stuff growing up. I genuinely didn't believe it until other people started vouching for the stories! He called his friend for a bit so the 3 of us just chatted at a table late at night. It was some fun times.

He made me go up the lighthouse that had a beehive next to it. Here's what that view looked like:

![image](https://cdn.hackclub.com/01a0791f-48ef-779b-bd40-11c1fd492b3f/PXL_20260904_030742803.gif)

I ended up going to sleep after, and then it was Friday!

## Day 3

Friday was a hectic day. Indy and I went over the Maritol and then talked about event logistics and big picture stuff for a couple of hours before we headed back over to Forbes Island to get some food

![image](https://cdn.hackclub.com/01a07922-9652-76da-be73-765b85e2ddc9/paste-1788738703496.png)

<div class="caption"> there were a bunch of instruments in this room! it was fun to mess around with while bouncing program ideas </div>

We explored some of the other boats parked again, and then we decided that we should probably go to civilization to get some supplies and also grab people if anyone was able to meet us there (we invited some people last minute, but we weren't sure if they were going to be able to come). We found some random pontoon boat parked nearby, and then took it to get to Starboard Home Marina, which has at least a _bit_ of civilization.

![image](https://cdn.hackclub.com/01a07929-3523-7d15-8b1b-96d3f12de744/PXL_20260904_220913922.gif)

<div class="caption"> this was a funny boat. </div>

This was an insanely stressful ride, we almost ran out of fuel and we had to keep tipping the gasoline canister at the right angle to actually get us there. Watching how much our fuel was bubbling for an 20 minutes straight is _not_ a fun experience. We were _this_ close to running out.

We got mexican food at some random restuarant nearby, and then went to Bethel Market to get some snacks. I got some sunglasses because the sun was killing my eyes.

![image](https://cdn.hackclub.com/01a0792f-4ac0-70dd-8420-aba5f2e0f6ad/paste-1788739535677.png)

<div class="caption"> the sun got me good </div>

Nobody we invited showed up, so we were about to head home when some guys at the marina recognized Indy. We decided to hang out for a bit; sadly I don't have pictures of this.

One thing that I found really interesting was that the people we met actually live on their boats full-time! They had a full studio inside complete with a couch, TV, computer, kitchen, etc. The only rent they pay is to park their boat by this marina. It was peering into a world I'd never come across before, which was super cool.

Anyhow, we decided to head back around 8:30, which would get us back to Forbes Island by 9:30 ish. A little late, but we've done late night expeditions before. Should be fine, I figured.

We were about to leave, but we needed to get gas somehow. Fortunately one of the people who live by the marina, Topher, had a 5 gallon tank. We paid him 30 bucks and put in the gas.

![image](https://cdn.hackclub.com/01a07935-516f-712d-b12e-828c1400b309/PXL_20260905_041647991.gif)

<div class="caption"> smooth sailing! </div>

![image](https://cdn.hackclub.com/01a07936-d601-72b3-8031-2609d64a34fc/paste-1788740031346.png)

<div class="caption"> pov you are stuck in the middle of the water </div>

_kch_... nothing.

_Indy, you have got to be kidding me_.

This went on for roughly 15 minutes before we realized we were SOL. _Insanely_ forunately for us though, the current happened to carry us in the exact direction of another dock. All we had to do was just wait until we got close enough before tying our boat down.

After assessing where the hell we ended up, we realized that the easiest thing to do would be to walk back to Starboard Home marina and sleep in one of the boats there, so we ended up trekking for an hour to get back.

![image](https://cdn.hackclub.com/01a0793a-b3de-70f1-94c2-03cf82e58b97/paste-1788740284017.png)

We cut our walk short by 20 minutes and Uber'd the rest of the way there. We got to the marina, and then pretty much immediately crashed in a boat that belonged to a friend of Indy's.

## Day 4

It was now Saturday. I woke up a bit earlier than Indy, so I first went to the gas station to get toothbrushes and some breakfast.

![image](https://cdn.hackclub.com/01a07940-fdfa-70d4-a0be-11e7f701bf7e/paste-1788740695851.png)

<div class="caption"> the fact this could be considered food should be illegal. this was truly awful </div>

A short while later, Indy got up and we were now tryign to figure out how to get the hell back. All of our stuff was still on Forbes, all I had was my phone and jacket.

Step one was getting back to the boat. We once again pestered Topher and asked if he could come out to the boat and see if he could figure out what was wrong; ideal scenario is he figures out our issue right where we docked it, and then we could just take our boat back to Forbes Island!

Sadly, despite lots of starter fluid and debugging, it seems like there was a deeper problem with our engine. We tied up the our pontoon boat to the boat Topher brought us in, and then brought it back to the marina.

![image](https://cdn.hackclub.com/01a0794c-0b51-71be-b60f-0794c8927a33/PXL_20260905_191111351.gif)

<div class="caption"> I will say, I felt like a pirate with this setup! </div>

Once we got back to the marina, Topher brought in his friend and tried debugging the engine for a bit. Eventually they concluded that it definitely wasn't a quick fix and so we've have to leave the boat there for them to repair.

Indy decided that from there the best plan is probably for us to take the tow boat out to Forbes, haul our food and supplies back to the Starboard Home marina, and then take an Uber to Antioch where we could BART back to wherever we were crashing in the Bay. This seemed good like a good plan.

Our tow boat was out of gas, so the first thing we needed to do was get a refill. Topher once again saved our asses as he had some spare canisters, and then drove us in his land cruiser to the gas station and back.

I wish I took a picture of this car. It was seriously cool. Looked something like this, but with camo prints and awesome tires:

![image](https://cdn.hackclub.com/01a07952-7b51-7193-a4d6-223a05e3ef73/paste-1788741842349.png)

Anyways, after dumping 10 gallons of gas, off we went back to Forbes Island!

I did a final tour of the island, so I took a bunch of videos and photos of all the rooms for future reference. I'll update this blog later to with a drive of them, probably.

While taking these photos, I ran into some folks on the Maritol, so I ended up talking with them for a bit, which was pretty cool.

ANYWAYS. After loading our stuff back onto the boat and triple checking we didn't lose anything, we left Forbes Island!

![image](https://cdn.hackclub.com/01a07957-be07-7b16-a9df-0bea9627284b/PXL_20260906_004501060.gif)

<div class="caption"> goodbye island... you will be missed (?) </div>

We stopped at the marina and gave them the rest of our food as a thank-you, and then off we went to the BART.

## Overall thoughts

Hectic is a word of all time to describe this trip. It was really humbling to have so many things go wrong. That being said, I feel so much more ready to run something on this island!

Some stuff of note:

- Bugs WILL fuck you up. I got so many bug bites all over my body I can't even count
- The sun also fucks you up. Even with sunscreen I still got pretty sunburnt and it was pretty cool
- I am never taking running water and functioning toilets for granted ever again

Some new skills I picked up:

- I can tie a boat to a dock!
- I now drive boats, somewhat
- I can now fish

I think oftentimes when we're in tech and adjacent spheres for so long we forget about what it's like to live out there in the real world, where the environment is always constantly trying to kill you. Every bit of infrastructure you use day to day like clean running water, shelter, air conditioning, etc was deliberately made by another person, and isn't a default in the world.
