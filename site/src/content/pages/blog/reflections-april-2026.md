I've had a number of learnings the past month but I don't think any of them are really worth their own blog post. Regardless I want them out there. Figured I'd put them here:

## Vermont

Vermont was a strange time. I went there after going to Campfire (hack club hackathon) in LA and spending a few days in SF thereafter. It was roughly 2 weeks.

It was a really big recallibration for me.

I realized how much I had fallen into the rabbit hole of fake work. I didn't realize how real / unreal hack club was.

## Symposium

I've always had a few gripes with Socratica, mainly the level of polish and intersection it has with the whole tech sphere.

Regardless, when I saw symposium was coming up, I was still pretty excited - there were cool things coming out of it, couldn't be bullshit

Shoutout my friends Daniel and Sammy for hosting me last minute at their airbnb. I deadass would've been homeless.

I decided to record a 1 minute interview with every single person.

I think sometimes I take places like Socratica for granted because of how immerse I am in Hack Club & that related environment

## Toronto

When I first moved here, I found Toronto rather boring and thought that any other city must be better. I wanted to spend as little time as possible here and so for my first semester of college I spent less than 8 weeks in the city. Every other week I was in Boston, London, SF, LA, \<insert location here\>, etc. Still passed my courses somehow!

I became increasingly pessimistic towards this place. _People here don't have passion_, _I feel so bored and uninspired here_

Cut to around

This city is actually great. I think I need to leave because ive lived here my whole life, but there's genuinely not that much I could ask for.

To rationalize it in my head I ended up following a very clear narrative of

tch, I grew increasingly pessmistic
