Name's a bit of a contention for me.

I've been called a handful of names over the course of my life. Alex, Robots4Lyfe (10 year old me was NOT it), Dari, qcoral, and most recently alexren in the Hack Club circle.

I've also had a lot of different chapters in my life. Hypixel, Overwatch, and 3D printing, among others. With each one I took on a bit of a different personality, had a different reptuation, and had a different social circle.

Most of these activites were terminally online (with the exception of origami & chess). When I moved to doing things in Real Life, I spent a _long_ time thinking about the next handle. _Who actually am I? What should I name myself?_ Questions like these took a non-negligbible amout of my mental capacity every day.

It's really easy to fall into the trap of thinking the name defines everything. After all, if it's a bad idea for brands to rename, surely it must be bad for you as well. Aren't we all walking brands? How do we square this dilemma?

The truth is is that identity is a transient property. It is not something that just gets switched on and off, nor is it something that stays permanent. It constantly evolves. It changes with each interaction you have, each thing you do.

What actually holds up is what you do and the people you meet. What you actually call yourself and brand yourself is free to morph around that over time, and how you do so is far more malleable than what you think.

Focus on doing. Change your names as you go
