This past year has been a strange one for me. Every social default in my life got eroded one way or the other. Took a leave from college, have been working remotely, etc.

I've spent my whole life putting up walls one way or another. Historically it's been gender related, but as of late I've found it more generalized through the other facets of my life. It's been really tiring.

Being friends with somebody is a lot of things. I think one of the main things is mutual understanding. You can't be understood if you won't let yourself be read.

Live, more genuinely.
