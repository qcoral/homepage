I keep needing to remind myself of this really simple fact time and time again. Keep taking more notes.

Making projects can be likened to climbing a cliff. You climb for a bit, set an anchor point, figure out where to go, and then keep climbing. Doing everything in one go is almost impossible.

Taking notes is analogous to setting those anchor points. It allows you to turn the passing thoughts you have into solid ideas, which give you a starting base to move from once again. Otherwise, your mind is left flailing about trying to do remember everything at once; this is certainly a recipe for disaster.

If you ever feel like you've been sitting on ideas for awhile but can't seem to execute on them, maybe it's time to put down an anchor.

Take more notes.
