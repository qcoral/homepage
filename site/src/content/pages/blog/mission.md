I decided to take a leave from college earlier this year. Whenever people ask me why, my usual answer is "job's not done." This of course, then asks the question, what _is_ the job, exactly?

Every time, I seem to give a slightly different story. To be truthfully honest, I don't fully know the answer myself. I think it's a couple of different things:

For one, I want to feel like I did my 9-year-old self right. The one who saw hardware projects for the first time and _wishes_ she had peers for it. The one who wanted the same world as I do now:

I want to live in a world where every day, there's a third space I can go to & make things. I want to live in a world where anyone in the world can find a group of peers tp design, build, and share creative projects with. I want to live in a world where every day I can wake up to thousands of videos by thousands of people about the thousands of projects they've created.

And moreover:

I want to live in a world where people spend as much time creating things as they do playing video games. I want to live in a more beautiful world. I want to live in a world where people step up for who they are. I want to live in a kinder world. I want to live in a world where people believe they have agency and that the world they exist in is malleable.

_Wouldn't that be so great?_

I'm not sure when the job will ever done; I think there will be a point where future me will feel alright with whatever they have done by then. 2 years down the line, 5 years down the line, even 10; maybe i'll go back to college then
