_should probably finish this at some point!_

tl;dr was really good at overwatch. played in tournaments & travelled. learned a lot from that time. Was being personally coached by [Crusty, perhaps the greatest Overwatch coach of all time](https://liquipedia.net/overwatch/Crusty)

Also was really good at hypixel bedwars, but I was an incredibly annoying 12 year old so if you know me from that time I am truly sorry.

main learnings:

- more time spent != more progress
- deliberate, targeted practice takes you very far
- your personal health matters so, so much.
- only focus on what you can control. you can either choose to spend a bunch of your energy in complaining, or you can redirect 100% of that into winning
- don't be afraid to put yourself out there - you cannot fear embarassment.
- what makes things special is the people (this one ended up being very pivotal for what would become my work at Hack Club!)
- most people you look up to are a conversation away.

One of the final learnings I was left with was that you _cannot_ run away from the past; In Jan 2023, I made the calculated decision to quit as I realized that no matter which way I spun it, being a pro gamer wasn't actually very desirable:

- Do 1 year of pro gaming on a gap year, then go to mid college and forever wish I was still back in the scene
- Go pro for 4-5 years, end up at 22 with no degree no skills outside of the game no friends outside of the game etc etc
- Go pro for 2-3 years and become a twitch streamer, even though I almost certainly didn't have the personality for that

and this was all absolute best case scenario - no matter how good I was there was always the chance I'd have trouble finding a team for one reason or another.

All that in mind, I decided to quit! The way I did so however, was by trying to burn all semblance of my old identity I could find and starting anew instead of letting it slowly ease away. Turns out this is really bad for you for a lot of reasons! I'll write about that at some point. For now you'll have to grab me irl and ask.
