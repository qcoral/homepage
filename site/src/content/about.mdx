### about!

Hi there! I'm Alex. I'm a 20-year old longtime maker, former esports player, and current maker advocate.

I spent 10 years growing up without a proper community to build & share physical projects with, so I'm currently building it out myself @ [Hack Club](https://hackclub.com).

Need to reach me? Find me at:

In the past, I [designed my own 3D printer](https://github.com/the-openary/neutrino) from scratch (that fits in a filament box!), ran a [4-day hardware hackathon](https://www.cbsnews.com/sanfrancisco/news/hack-club-hosts-teen-coders-san-francisco/) at GitHub HQ, and also played Overwatch semi-professionally.

I'm deeply interested in creating a third space for young people to make projects together, both online and in person.

If you ever see me in public, please say hi! I promise I don't bite.

**Some interesting projects I've cobbled together:**

- [Neutrino](https://github.com/the-openary/neutrino), a $200 USD printer that fits in a filament box!
- [kicad-gitignore](https://github.com/qcoral/kicad-gitignore), a plugin to generate .gitignore files for your KiCAD projects, since apparently that didn't exist yet.
- [Image paste CDN](https://marketplace.visualstudio.com/items?itemName=dari.paste-cdn), a VSCode plugin that replaces pasted markdown images with a [Hack Club CDN](https://cdn.hackclub.com) link instead. Up to 50GB free storage per person!
- [collectibles!](https://github.com/qcoral/collectibles), some free stuff u might grab from me if you meet me :D

**And here are some Hack Club projects I've led!**

- [Hackpad](https://hackpad.hackclub.com) - design your own macropad, get all the parts to make it! _1000+ macropads made to date._
- [Highway](https://highway.hackclub.com) - design _any_ hardware project, get up to $350 to build it! _$100k given out to 700+ teenagers in summer 2025_
- [Undercity](https://undercity.hackclub.com), a 200-person 4-day hardware hackathon @ GitHub HQ! [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/sanfrancisco/news/hack-club-hosts-teen-coders-san-francisco/) [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kaEFv7e49mo)

- [Infill](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5G_g6yMLbMs), design a 3D printer, get funding to build it -> build it, join our booth at RMRRF 2025!
- [Printing Legion](https://printlegion.hackclub.com), an international network of 3D printers for any teen to get their parts 3D printed. _399 projects printed so far!_
